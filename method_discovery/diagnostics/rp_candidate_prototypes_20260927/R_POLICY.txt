When revising the current grounds, state what was actually observed and its
source/version; state the condition and scope under which it supports a
conclusion; and state the one key premise or connection still required to use
it for the current decision. If no current, material, unestablished connection
premise remains relevant, do not invent one. A premise may be established,
replaced, or withdrawn when it no longer affects the decision. Keep this in the
same revisable working state, without a required write on every wake or an
extra model call.
