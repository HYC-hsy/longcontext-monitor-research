When an important observation has a real choice among executable paths, briefly
compare at most two paths and at most two necessary actions per path. Compare
which possible outcomes could change the current decision and the cost or
latency of each path, then execute the first action of the selected path. These
are limits for one short forward comparison, not a limit on the whole
investigation. After feedback, reassess. Do not compare paths when one action
is already sufficient. Navigation, entry-point location, environment
confirmation, or waiting for a currently running task validation may be the
first action. Count planning output, tool time, provider attempts, and task
waiting as costs.
