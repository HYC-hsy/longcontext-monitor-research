This note only clarifies the time at which interpretation_replaced targets must
be current; the four fixed spec files, mechanisms and budgets are unchanged.

A new or materially changed retirement relationship must target an exact,
current, active Requirement head in the candidate, with valid source,
task_version and no self-reference or cycle. Copying an old relation to a
different logical Requirement is a new relationship.

An already committed, unchanged relationship retains its original exact
replacement RequirementRef. Later revisions or retirement of that replacement
do not rewrite the earlier interpretation. Its exact historical body must
still exist in the immutable archive or current head; absent history is not
invented. Unrelated changes to the original Requirement do not by themselves
make unchanged canonical retirement content into a new relationship.

The classification compares the frozen base and candidate by logical
requirement_id and canonical retirement content, not a model-supplied label.
Builder and transactional CAS apply the same check. A stale base or goal epoch
fails; the check is not reclassified against a newer base mid-commit.
