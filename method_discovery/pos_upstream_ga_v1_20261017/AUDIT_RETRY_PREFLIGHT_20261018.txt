Return-count clarification
--------------------------

neither message_stop nor stop_reason, and also marks max_tokens separately.
The request wrapper emits provider_response outcome=success after the parser
returns its blocks, even when those blocks are the incomplete-stream warning.
This is a capture/outcome convention, not permission to execute an invented
answer. agent_loop identifies the warning as provider_error, selects no
action and records PROVIDER_FAILURE. No runtime parser/transport change was
made. In the old compressor Task archive there are 125 logical requests,
125 observable attempts and 125 return records. Offline classification
finds one explicit incomplete-stream marker. The other 124 lack separately
captured stop_reason/message_stop, so their terminal completeness is
conservatively unknown in this archive-only classification; their actual
tools/observations remain in the original records. The five zero-model
classification tests cover explicit complete termination, missing terminal
proof, incomplete sentinel despite outcome=success, provider error and
max_tokens. Test command/stdout/stderr and aggregate result are in
raw_retry_preflight_v2/offline_tests and old_compressor/return_classification.json.
Historical outcome=success events were not rewritten.
