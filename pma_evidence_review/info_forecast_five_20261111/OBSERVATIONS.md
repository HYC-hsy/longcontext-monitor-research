All statements here refer to the archived visible records. A public explanation is not private reasoning, a saved plan is not a verified future fact, and a later action is not proof that a reminder caused it. Full text is in `memory_payload.jsonl`, `memory_response.jsonl`, `memory_tool_results.jsonl`, `memory_cycle.jsonl`, `memory.json` and the Task trace within the ZIP.
