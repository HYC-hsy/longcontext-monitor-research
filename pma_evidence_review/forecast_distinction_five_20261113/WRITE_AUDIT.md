Scope and coding: one row is one successful write, **not one fact**; a row may contain several claims. “Explicit distinction” means the public text at or before the write names a concrete supported finding *and* a concrete tentative alternative/inference and keeps the latter out of the write. “Confirmed-only explanation” describes a prospective use and calls the selected material confirmed, but does not show a concrete contrast; that is not automatically wrong when no alternative is visible. “Insufficient public process” means the same response and earlier responses in that wake do not provide enough substantive public explanation *before* the write.
